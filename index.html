Wristbridge

Wristbridge helps a Galaxy Watch work with an iPhone. Notifications, call decline and music controls run from the Wristbridge app on your watch. The iPhone app guides setup.

Beta. Wristbridge is being tested. Features that are not built yet, such as answering calls and Apple Health, are not available and nothing is for sale.

How it works

Your watch pairs with your iPhone in iOS Settings, like any Bluetooth watch. Your iPhone then shares its notifications with the watch, and the Wristbridge watch app shows them. No account and no server are involved.
