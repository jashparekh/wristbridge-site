Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026. This covers the Wristbridge iPhone app and the Wristbridge watch app.

The short version

Wristbridge does not have accounts and does not run servers. It does not upload your notifications, contacts, photos or health data to us.

What the apps do with your data

  • Notifications. Your iPhone shares notifications with your watch over Bluetooth using Apple's built-in notification service. The Wristbridge watch app receives them and shows them on the watch. The Wristbridge iPhone app does not read your notifications.
  • On your watch. The watch app keeps a list of the apps that have sent you notifications, so you can mute them, and your settings (for example hiding message text and quiet hours). It also keeps a short log of events such as connections and counts. The log holds no notification text, contact names or app names. These stay on the watch until you clear them or uninstall the app.
  • On your iPhone. The iPhone app stores which setup steps you have ticked. This stays on the iPhone.
  • Bluetooth. The iPhone app uses Bluetooth only to find and connect to the watch you choose, for the optional app link. It does not scan for or collect information about other devices.

What we collect

Nothing. The apps have no analytics, advertising or tracking, and send nothing to us.

Other companies

Apple provides TestFlight, which can collect crash reports and tester feedback if you choose to share them, under Apple's own privacy policy. Google Play distributes the watch app, under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your notification content from either.

Children

Wristbridge is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

You can mute apps, hide message text or switch off the watch app at any time, and removing the app deletes its data from that device. You can stop sharing notifications in iPhone Settings › Bluetooth › the info icon next to your watch.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. If the apps start to collect or send data, we will say so here first.
