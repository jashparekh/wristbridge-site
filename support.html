Support

For the Wristbridge beta. Email accounts@jashparekh.com and tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, your watch model, and what you saw on each screen.

Set up

  1. Install Wristbridge on your watch (from the link you were sent) and open it.
  2. On the watch, tap Get started and allow Bluetooth and notifications.
  3. Pair the watch in iPhone Settings › Bluetooth. On the watch, open Settings › Connections › Bluetooth › Pair new device and leave it open. On the iPhone, tap the watch under Other Devices and accept the code on both screens.
  4. The watch chooses your iPhone by itself when only one is paired. If two iPhones are paired it asks you to pick. This can take up to 30 seconds.
  5. Tap Allow when your iPhone asks to share notifications with the watch.
  6. Send yourself a message from an app that is not muted, with the watch on your wrist and its screen on.

Fixes

The watch says "Looking for your iPhone" and never connects.
Paired this watch before? Forget it on the iPhone (Settings › Bluetooth › the info icon › Forget This Device). The watch clears its own record by itself within a minute. Then pair again. If the watch list shows two entries named iPhone, tap Choose a different iPhone and try the other one.
There is no Share System Notifications switch.
iOS only shows it after the watch has connected and asked for notifications. Go back to choosing your iPhone on the watch.
A message arrived but nothing popped up.
Check that the watch is on your wrist, the app is not muted in the watch's Notification settings, and Do Not Disturb is off on the watch.
Notifications stopped after a restart.
Open Wristbridge on the watch and tap Try now. If that fails, forget and pair again as above.

Linking the iPhone app (optional)

  1. On the watch, open Wristbridge, tap Pair the iPhone app, then tap Start. The watch stays visible for 2 minutes.
  2. On the iPhone, open Link apps and tap Find my watch.
  3. Compare the six-digit code on both screens and tap Codes match on both. If they differ, tap They differ.

It reconnects by itself afterwards while the iPhone app is allowed to run in the background. If you swipe the app away on the iPhone, open it again. If the iPhone says the watch doesn't recognise it, tap Forget this watch and pair again, with the watch's Start open.

The watch shows the wrong time

A watch used only with an iPhone can stay on GMT, and many models offer no way to change the time zone. Wristbridge cannot change it. The apps use your iPhone's time for quiet hours and hide notification times that would be wrong.

What is not built yet

Answering calls and Apple Health are still being built. The iPhone app cannot see whether notifications are reaching your watch; that status is shown on the watch.
